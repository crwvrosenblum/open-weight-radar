How to read these rumors

Green identifies an official preview; amber identifies training or teaser evidence; red identifies unverified reports, listings, and speculation. A listing may be a placeholder and does not prove a model exists or will have open weights. Dates are attributed claims, not forecasts. Each model links to dated evidence; source review dates are separate from automated news checks. New named candidates are discovered from recent headlines and stay unverified. Matches with weight-bearing repositories from the model’s own Hugging Face organization are removed from this section. An absence from this bounded snapshot is not proof that weights are unavailable.

Coverage and methodology

The snapshot combines the first 60 trending text-generation and image-text-to-text results with each tracked lab’s 60 most recently created repositories. It includes text-generation, text2text-generation, image-text-to-text, and any-to-any models with public metadata and at least one weight file; adapter-only repositories are excluded. Quantized variants and fine-tunes may appear as distinct repositories. This is a bounded sample, not a complete catalog of open models. Downloads are rolling 30-day counts; likes are cumulative; trending score is Hugging Face’s own momentum signal. Rank changes compare positions in the sampled trending list at consecutive successful builds; “New” means newly observed in that list. Rank movement starts after the first snapshot.

Tracked labs: {{COVERAGE}}. Open-weight does not imply an open-source or commercially permissive license. Gated weights may require accepting terms or requesting access.
